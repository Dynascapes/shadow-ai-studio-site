## rules

```text
Quote Intake Referrals rules

This server is for referral leads for Quote Intake Studio.

Rules:
- Do not mass-DM random strangers or spam servers.
- Only message people you know, business owners you have a real reason to contact, or people publicly asking for website/business help.
- Clearly say you may get a referral payout if they become a client.
- Do not use fake claims, fake screenshots, fake reviews, or fake guarantees.
- Do not promise prices, timelines, results, discounts, or payouts.
- Do not pretend to be the owner.
- Use public contact info only.
- Keep private client/payment info out of public channels.
- Duplicate, fake, irrelevant, or low-quality referrals are not payable.
- Payouts only happen after a referred business becomes a paid client.
- Referred businesses may get $10 off their first small fix after manual approval.
- One referral credit per real business. Credits are not stackable.
```

## how-it-works

```text
How referrals work

Refer local service businesses that may be open to help with their website, contact page, or quote request page.

You only earn if your referred business becomes a paid Quote Intake Studio client.

Steps:
1. Find someone you know, a business owner you have a real reason to contact, or someone publicly asking for website/business help.
2. Send the approved message.
3. If they say they are open to a quick look, submit the referral.
4. Quote Intake Studio reviews and follows up.
5. If they become a paid client, you earn the active bounty.

Status flow:
Submitted -> Approved -> Contacted -> Client won -> Paid

Referral link:
Use /referral-link to get your link. Ask the business to mention your Discord username or referral code when they email.

Example submission:
Business: Example Plumbing
Website: exampleplumbing.com
Who replied: Owner replied on Instagram DM
Interest proof: "Yeah send me the site. Our quote form is annoying."
Why it counts: real business, public website, clear interest.

Do not submit random business lists. Raw leads and mass-DM replies do not count.
```
