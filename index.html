FAQ

Short answers before you send a project request.

What do customers receive?

Deliverables depend on the scope. Common deliverables include website files, automation setup notes, bug-fix summaries, deployment notes, audit reports, and next-step recommendations.

Do you work with AI-built apps?

Yes. Shadow AI Studio can review and fix AI-built websites, prototypes, and apps that need debugging, deployment, forms, payments, database setup, or launch cleanup.

Can I cancel after paying?

You can request cancellation before work starts. Once work begins, refunds are reviewed based on completed work and the agreed scope.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Most work is fixed-scope. Ongoing maintenance can be quoted separately after the initial project is reviewed.
