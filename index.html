Conversion Fixes

Example scopes for realistic, fixed-scope troubleshooting. No hourly bloat, no fake testimonials.

Example Scope

Lost Missed-Call Leads

Problem: Callers hang up when you're busy on a job. They immediately search for a competitor who answers.

Fix path: Set up a simple missed-call auto-text reply explaining you're on a job and asking what service they need.

Example Scope

Buried Mobile Quote Button

Problem: Customers can't find how to get a quote on their phones because buttons are small or require deep scrolling.

Fix path: Add a high-visibility, thumb-friendly sticky header or footer button that jumps straight to the intake form.

Example Scope

Broken Form Notifications

Problem: Customers fill out your quote form but emails fail to send, leaving leads sitting unread in your inbox for days.

Fix path: Update field mapping, fix SMTP delivery, test notification routing, and set up a secondary backup channel.

FAQ

Short answers before you send a project request.

What do customers receive?

Deliverables depend on the package. You receive ready-to-use email/SMS copy templates, custom responsive forms, sticky mobile CTA code, or an actionable website lead-leak checklist.

Do you work with my existing website?

Yes. Quote Intake Studio can implement mobile CTAs, custom forms, and auto-responder notifications on WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Shopify, or custom HTML sites.

Can I cancel after requesting a review?

Yes. Since we confirm scope before payment, there is no risk of booking a project that isn't a fit. Revisions are included to ensure your forms or buttons function perfectly.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Most work is fixed-scope. We deliver clean code and copy templates that are easy to maintain yourself. Ongoing updates can be quoted separately as needed.
