Client Snapshot

Sample business: a local service company that receives leads through a website form, phone number, Google Business Profile, and Instagram. The site looks credible, but the follow-up path is slow and several conversion points are easy to miss on mobile.

  • Primary goal: turn more form visitors and missed callers into booked consultations.
  • Main risk: leads wait too long for a reply and contact competitors.
  • Recommended first fix: instant text/email follow-up for every form submission.

Priority Findings

1. No instant follow-up after the contact form

The form appears to send an email, but the visitor does not get a clear next step or a fast confirmation. This is the biggest leak because the lead is warm at the moment they submit the form.

  • Impact: high
  • Fix: send an instant confirmation email or SMS with expected response time and booking link.
  • Proof needed: test form submission screenshot and inbox confirmation.

2. Mobile call-to-action is too low on the page

On mobile, the strongest action button appears after too much scrolling. A visitor who is ready to call or request pricing may leave before reaching it.

  • Impact: medium-high
  • Fix: add a visible top-screen call or quote button and repeat it after service sections.
  • Proof needed: mobile screenshots at 390px and 768px widths.

3. Missed-call fallback is not explained

If staff misses a call, the site does not tell visitors what happens next. A simple missed-call text-back system can recover leads without adding a full AI receptionist.

  • Impact: medium
  • Fix: add a text-back workflow or manual call-back process with a clear response window.
  • Proof needed: call routing note or phone provider capability check.

4. Reviews are separated from the request path

Reviews exist, but they are not close enough to the contact buttons. Put two or three short proof points near the form to reduce hesitation.

  • Impact: medium
  • Fix: move a short review strip near the main form and add a review request flow after service is completed.
  • Proof needed: approved public reviews or client-provided testimonials.

5. No written follow-up sequence

Staff may follow up manually, but there is no visible script or timing plan. A basic sequence keeps the business from forgetting leads during busy days.

  • Impact: high if lead volume is steady
  • Fix: use a 3-touch follow-up sequence over 48 hours.
  • Proof needed: approved message copy and opt-out language where required.

48-Hour Fix Plan

  • Day 1: test form, capture screenshots, confirm where lead notifications go, and add a response-time note.
  • Day 1: write the first confirmation email/SMS and one missed-call reply.
  • Day 2: move or duplicate the mobile call-to-action near the top of the page.
  • Day 2: add two review snippets near the form and document the next follow-up steps.

Starter Message Templates

Form confirmation:

Thanks for reaching out. We got your request and will reply soon. If this is urgent, call us at [phone]. If you prefer text, reply here with the best time to contact you.

Missed-call text-back:

Sorry we missed your call. This is [business name]. What service do you need help with, and what is the best time to reach you today?

Review request:

Thanks for choosing us. If the service went well, could you leave a quick review here: [review link]. It helps local customers know what to expect.

Async Process

This audit can be delivered without a call. The client sends a website URL, screenshots, the main offer, and any safe public links. Passwords, payment details, private customer data, and account recovery details are not needed for the first audit.

  • Input: website URL, target service, contact method, screenshots if useful.
  • Output: prioritized findings, fix plan, message templates, and next-step checklist.
  • Optional next step: implement the simple page/contact-flow fixes after scope approval.
